# azir: The ACIR Visualizer for Noir Circuits

A visualizer which fetches your the ACIR and Brillig opcodes from your Compiled Noir circuits, and illustrates how they're translated to R1CS-ish and PLONK-ish constraints.


### Current Features
- Compiled bytecode to Opcodes

### Future Additions
- Visualising ACIR constraints through Directed Acyclic Graphs
- Illustration of translation of DAG nodes to R1CS-ish and PLONK-ish constraints used by different backends.
